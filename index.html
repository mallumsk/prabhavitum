Who We Are

Prabhavitum Science PU College, established in 2019–20 in Maski, Karnataka, is committed to providing quality Pre-University education in Science and Commerce. We specialize in supporting rural-background students with strong academics, integrated coaching for competitive exams, and personalized mentoring. Our goal is to nurture talent, build confidence, and prepare students for academic excellence and successful careers.

Our Vision

To empower rural students through quality education and guide them toward academic excellence and successful careers in Science and Commerce.

Our Mission

  • • Provide strong conceptual learning in Science & Commerce
  • • Support rural students with personalized mentorship
  • • Prepare students for board and competitive exams
  • • Maintain a disciplined, student-focused environment
